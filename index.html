Ravikiran Rajagopal

Product Manager · Builder · Lifelong Learner

Ravikiran Rajagopal is a Director of Product Management (Technology) specializing in digital platform transformation, advanced A/B experimentation, and data-driven product strategy across Parks & Entertainment, Financial Services, and Consumer sectors.

At SeaWorld Parks & Entertainment he has delivered $18M+ in documented business impact — reducing checkout failure rates from 20% to 5%, executing 250+ A/B experiments across 12 branded websites, and driving $10M+ annual ROI from an enterprise experimentation program he designed from the ground up.

His work has been published and peer-reviewed: he is a co-author of “Power Analysis is Essential” (Econ Journal Watch, Vol. 23, No. 1, March 2026) alongside Nobel laureate Guido Imbens, Andrew Gelman, and Ron Kohavi, and a Co-Investigator on an AEA-registered randomized controlled trial spanning 2.9 million website visitors.

Earlier, at Prudential Financial, he shipped the industry’s first native mobile retirement app (844K users) and first Alexa skill for retirement account management. He is a two-time industry award winner (Optimizely North America, Quantum Metric) and a judge for Globee Awards and the UConn Entrepreneurship Bootcamp for Veterans.
