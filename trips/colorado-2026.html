📋 Colorado Road Trip · Full Detail

Jul 31 – Aug 9, 2026 · complete day-by-day plan

Friday, July 31
  • Flight lands, arrive in Denver
  • Check-in to Sleep Inn & Suites Denver Int'l Airport (fully prepaid)
Saturday, August 1
  • 30 mins drive to Edgewater
  • Lunch in Gladys in Edgewater
  • 45 mins drive to Boulder
  • Shop in Costco, Whole Foods, etc.
  • Sightseeing in Boulder if time permits?
  • Dinner in Lotus Moon or Vegan Thai in Boulder
  • 1 hour drive to Estes Park
  • Check-in after 3:30pm to Alpine Trail Ridge Inn in Estes Park (fully prepaid)
    • Front-desk closes at 8pm; they need to be notified for late night
Sunday, August 2
  • Bear Lake Corridor in RMNP
    • Need Timed Entry+ Bear Lake Road reservations (opens 7pm the day before)
    • Or need to enter before 9am or after 2pm
  • Lunch pickup in Scratch or Notchtop Bakery
  • Nymph, Dream, Emerald, Haiyaha Lakes hike: 5 miles, moderate
    • Alpine lakes
  • Bear Lake hike: 0.7 miles, easy
  • Sprague Lake hike: 0.8 miles, easy
  • Dinner in Estes: Nepal's Café, Himalayan Curry, or Estes Thai
Monday, August 3
  • Bear Lake Corridor in RMNP
    • Need Timed Entry+ Bear Lake Road reservations (opens 7pm the day before)
    • Or need to enter before 9am or after 2pm
  • Lunch pickup in Scratch or Notchtop Bakery
  • Cub Lake hike: 5.2 or 6.2 miles, moderate
    • Moraine Park; wildlife viewing
    • Sunset?
  • Old Fall River Road onwards
    • Trail Ridge Road return
      • Sunset?
  • Visitor Center: Alpine or Beaver Meadows
  • Dinner in Estes: Nepal's Café, Himalayan Curry, or Estes Thai
Tuesday, August 4
  • Non-bear lake corridor in RMNP
    • Need Timed Entry reservations (opens 7pm the day before)
    • Or need to enter before 9am or after 2pm
  • Check-out by 10:30am from stay in Estes Park
  • Lunch pickup in Scratch or Notchtop Bakery
  • 1.5 hour drive to Grand Lake
    • Trail Ridge Road
      • Tundra communities hike: 1.1 miles, easy
    • Holzwarth Historic Site? Can skip
    • Kawuneeche Valley
      • Wildlife viewing
    • Adams Falls hike
  • 2.5 hour drive to Glenwood Springs
    • Hanging Lake trail: 3.1 miles, hard
  • Check-in after 3pm to stay in Glenwood Springs (pay at hotel)
    • Need to contact property if checking in after 9pm
  • Dinner in Glenwood Springs: Masala & Curry or Zheng Asian Bistro
Wednesday, August 5
  • Free breakfast in hotel
  • Lunch pickup in Bluebird Café?
  • 1 hour drive to Maroon Bells Scenic Area
    • Needs reservation for parking a vehicle or need to take a shuttle
  • Crater Lake hike: 3.7 miles, moderate
  • 1 hour drive back to Glenwood Springs
  • Iron Mountain Hot Springs or Glenwood Hot Springs Pool
  • Dinner in Glenwood Springs: Masala & Curry or Zheng Asian Bistro
Thursday, August 6
  • Free breakfast in hotel
  • Check-out by 11am from stay in Glenwood Springs
  • Lunch pickup in Bluebird Café?
  • 2.5 hour drive to North Rim of BCGNP
    • Exclamation Point hike: 3 miles, easy
    • Chase View hike: 0.6 miles, moderate
    • Black Canyon Narrows View
    • Balanced Rock View
  • 1.5 hour drive to Montrose
  • Dinner in Montrose: Camp Robber or Urban Hip-E+
  • Check-in after 3pm (before midnight) to stay in Montrose (fully prepaid)
Friday, August 7
  • Free breakfast in hotel
  • Check-out by 11am from stay in Montrose
  • 45 mins drive to South Rim of BCGNP
  • Pack sandwiches for lunch
  • Black Canyon Scenic Drive
    • Warner Point hike: 1.6 miles, moderate
    • Oak Flat Loop hike: 1.5 miles, moderate
      • Or Oak Flat, Uplands, and Rim Rock Loop hike: 3 miles, moderate
    • Gunnison Point Overlook
    • Painted Wall View
    • Pulpit Rock Overlook
  • 4.5 hour drive to Colorado Springs
  • Dinner in The Burrowing Owl or Santana's Vegan Grill in Colorado Springs
  • Check-in to stay in Colorado Springs
Saturday, August 8
  • Free breakfast in hotel
  • 1 hour drive to Pikes Peak (seniors can skip)
    • Summit Loop hike: 0.7 miles, easy
  • 1 hour drive back to Colorado Springs
  • Lunch in The Burrowing Owl or Santana's Vegan Grill in Colorado Springs
  • Garden of the Gods
    • Palmer, Cabin Canyon, Strausenbach, and Central Gardens Loop hike: 4.3 miles, moderate
    • Siamese Twins Loop hike: 0.6 miles, easy
  • Dinner in The Burrowing Owl or Santana's Vegan Grill in Colorado Springs
Sunday, August 9
  • Free breakfast in hotel
  • Check-out by noon from stay in Colorado Springs
  • 1.25 hour drive to Edgewater
  • Lunch in Gladys in Edgewater
  • 30 mins drive to Denver airport
  • Flight departs around 4:30pm
